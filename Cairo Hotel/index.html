Established in 1999, Cairo Hotel & Spa has been a beacon of refined hospitality, offering guests an unparalleled experience that blends contemporary luxury with classical grace.

Nestled in the cultural heart of the city, our property features 120 meticulously appointed rooms and suites, three world-class restaurants, and a renowned spa — all designed to create moments of pure indulgence.
